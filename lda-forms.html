Core Technical Highlights of LDA Building & Zoning Regulations 2026

Mandatory statutory guidelines extracted from the official Gazette for legal compliance.

Maximum Heights & Storeys

Standard residential plots permit up to 38 ft (3 storeys) on roads up to 25 ft ROW[cite: 3]. For plots of 10 Marla and 1 Kanal, maximum permissible height extends to 45 ft (4 storeys, excluding basement) with a minimum clear storey height of 9 ft 6 inches[cite: 3, 5].

Mandatory Open Spaces & Setbacks

For plots below 10 Marla, a mandatory 5-ft rear open space is strictly enforced[cite: 8]. For 1 Kanal properties, a 7-ft rear space and 5-ft side passage on both sides are legally required for ventilation and natural lighting[cite: 8].

Underground Water Tank Capacities

Underground tanks are legally mandatory: 5 Marla homes require a minimum of 18 cu.ft (135 Gal)[cite: 17], 7 to 10 Marla requires 60 to 124 cu.ft[cite: 17], and 1 Kanal residential units require at least 200 cu.ft (1,200 Gal) capacity[cite: 17].

Rainwater Harvesting & Recharge Pits

Rooftop rainwater harvesting systems are mandatory for all plots 5 Marla and above[cite: 18]. Houses measuring 15 Marla or greater must construct an on-site aquifer recharge well/pit to preserve underground water tables[cite: 18].

Plot Amalgamation Rules

Two or more adjacent plots of the same land use category may be amalgamated side-by-side or back-to-back[cite: 2, 14]. The building regulations of the resultant aggregate plot size become applicable to the development[cite: 5].

Subdivision Restrictions

Subdivision is strictly prohibited for plots smaller than 1 Kanal in approved housing schemes[cite: 15]. For 2 Kanal plots, subdivision is permitted provided the resultant divided plots are at least 1 Kanal with a minimum front width of 35 ft[cite: 15].
